PocketSword Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy covers the PocketSword app for iPhone and iPad, published by Logos Hub Ltd. For our website, see the website Privacy Policy.

Data we collect

None. PocketSword has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting. Logos Hub runs no server for the app and receives no data from it.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your bookmarks and bookmark folders, reading history, settings, downloaded Bible modules and the search indexes built from them. This stays on your device and the app does not send it anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

Servers the app contacts

The app contacts a server only when you download or browse modules. Each request is a normal web request that includes your device's IP address and the file requested. The servers are:

  • ebible.org, for the Bible catalogue and Bible files;
  • crosswire.org, for the SWORD module catalogue and module packages; and
  • any module source you add yourself, which receives requests at the address you entered.

These services handle those requests under their own privacy practices. No other third-party service handles your data.

Module Maintainer Mode

This mode is off by default and is meant for module authors. When you turn it on, the app starts a small web server on your local Wi-Fi network so you can upload a module from a browser. It is reachable only on that network and stops when you turn the mode off. iOS asks for Local Network permission first.

Your choices and deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. You can remove bookmarks and downloaded modules inside the app, or delete the app to erase everything it stores. You can decline the Local Network permission at any time.

Children

PocketSword is a general Bible reader and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to us through the support page.
