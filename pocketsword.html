PocketSword

A free, open-source Bible reader and study app for iPhone and iPad.

PocketSword is built on the CrossWire SWORD engine and revives the original work of Nic Carter and the CrossWire Bible Society. It is published by Logos Hub Ltd, a non-profit based in Australia.

What it does

  • Read and search Bibles and study modules.
  • Download Bibles from eBible.org and modules from CrossWire, or add your own module source.
  • Keep bookmarks, bookmark folders and your reading history.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Your bookmarks, reading history and settings stay on your device.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad running iOS 17.6 or later.
