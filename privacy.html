LOGOS HUB

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Logos Hub is a public, non-profit digital library for the collection, preservation, and open distribution of the Bible, open-source Bible translations, and related study resources. This policy explains how this website (www.logoshub.org) handles information about the people who visit it.

In short: you do not need an account to use this site, we do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell your information.

Information we receive

This website does not ask you to enter personal information. The only information we receive directly from you is what you choose to send us, for example when you email us using the Contact Us link. That may include your name, your email address and the contents of your message.

Technical information

Like most websites, the service that hosts this site may automatically log technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the date and time of the request. This is used to operate and secure the site and is handled under the hosting provider's own privacy practices.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not currently set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes, we will update this policy before the change takes effect.

Third-party services

This site loads the Noto Serif typeface from Google Fonts. When your browser requests the font, it connects to Google's servers, and Google may receive your IP address and browser information. You can read more in Google's privacy policy.

How we use information

We use the information you send us only to:

  • respond to your questions, feedback or requests;
  • keep the website running, secure and working properly; and
  • meet legal obligations where required.

We do not sell your personal information or share it with third parties for their marketing.

Retention and security

We keep emails and the details in them only for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry or to meet legal requirements. We take reasonable steps to protect information we hold, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing us.

Links to other sites

This site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to us by email.
